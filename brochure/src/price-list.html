Price list  —  terms

What the rate carries, and what it does not.

Included in the rate
  • Design development and the cutting drawing
  • Master sketch and a rendering in your own space
  • Hand-cutting and setting at the bench
  • Mesh backing, numbered panels and a setting-out key
  • Crating and insured worldwide shipping
  • Spare tesserae from the same batch
Not included
  • Installation, unless fitted by our own team
  • Waterproofing, adhesive and grout
  • Site preparation and making good
  • Duties and local taxes where they apply
What moves the rate
Tessera scale
Finer cutting is more hours in the same square metre
Colour count
More stones in a palette means more sorting and more waste
Metal leaf
24k gold, white gold and copper are priced on the area used
Area
Larger commissions carry a lower rate per m²

Rates are quoted against an approved drawing and hold for the commission — there is no square-metre creep once the drawing is signed off. A fixed quotation follows the master sketch, at no cost and with no obligation.
