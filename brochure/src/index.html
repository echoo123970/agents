The craft

Everything is decided by hand, then held to a drawing.

Stone

Blocks are selected for vein direction before a single cut is made, so a figure runs through the composition rather than across it. The edge you see is a cut edge, not a moulded one.

Glass

Hand-poured glass carries colour through its full body. Gold and white-gold leaf are laid between glass layers, which is why the metal keeps its lustre in wet areas.

Setting

The flow of the setting lines — the andamento — is drawn before assembly. It is the difference between a picture made of tiles and a mosaic that moves with the light.

Technical  ·  III

Installation, site & care.

Mesh-backed panels are fixed by any competent tiling contractor. These are the conditions the work needs from the site, and the regime that keeps it looking the way it did on hand-over.

ItemRequirement
SubstrateRequired. Clean, flat and structurally sound; dry and free of contaminants before any mosaic is fixed
WaterproofingRequired in showers, hammams, fountains and pools: a compliant tanking system, applied and tested first
AdhesiveEpoxy adhesive recommended, and required for wet, outdoor and submerged areas. White adhesive is essential beneath translucent glass and pale stone
FixingFull bed, back-buttered on larger panels, mesh pressed fully into the adhesive. Floating method where a design mixes materials of different thickness
GroutMandatory in wet and outdoor areas. Epoxy grout recommended throughout — non-porous and chemical-resistant
Movement jointsMandatory. Carried through the mosaic to the project's own layout, with silicone at every change of plane
Slip resistanceDCOF ≥ 0.42 interior wet and ≥ 0.60 exterior, to local code. Honed (R11) rather than polished (R10) where a floor gets wet
SealingRequired on marble, particularly in wet and exterior areas, after grouting
CuringFull adhesive cure before grouting, and before filling pools or fountains — allow 14–28 days depending on system and conditions
Site conditionsA controlled environment: avoid extreme temperatures during fixing and curing, and protect from direct sun and draught
ProtectionCovered and protected from following trades until hand-over; final clean by the installing team
DocumentationCutting drawing, numbered installation key, material schedule and maintenance sheet issued with every delivery
Recommended

pH-neutral stone or glass cleaners for routine work; a two-in-one colour enhancer and sealer once a year on natural stone; spills wiped promptly — most staining is a spill that was left.

Avoid

Acidic cleaners, which etch natural stone permanently; abrasive pads and powders, which dull polished finishes and mark metal leaf; bleach and ammonia, which discolour grout and surfaces alike.

From idea to installation

Seven stages, one team, no hand-offs.

01

Brief

Drawings, dimensions, references and the feeling you are after. A site visit or a video walk-through where possible.

02

Design & free rendering

The studio develops the composition and returns a rendering of the mosaic in your own space at no cost, with alternatives on palette and tessera scale.

03

Material lay-up

The actual stones and glass are cut and laid up physically, photographed and sent — or presented in person — for sign-off.

04

Cutting drawing

Setting-out lines, panel divisions and a numbered key are issued, coordinated with the site programme.

05

Atelier production

Cutting and setting by hand at the bench, photographed at agreed stages so the client sees the work as it grows.

06

Delivery

Crated by panel sequence with the installation key, material schedule and spare tesserae from the same batch.

07

Installation

Fitted by our own team, or by your contractor with our drawings and remote supervision. Final clean, seal and hand-over.
