The atelier

Where Roman Craft Meets
Modern Design

For over two thousand years, mosaic has transformed architecture through the meticulous placement of individual pieces of stone and glass. We preserve that traditional craftsmanship while adapting it to the scale, precision, and creative requirements of contemporary spaces.

Every project is made to order. Starting from a concept, artwork, logo, pattern, or architectural drawing, our designers develop a custom mosaic tailored to the project's exact dimensions, shape, colors, materials, and application.

Our artisans then bring the design to life one tessera at a time, using natural marble and glass to create pieces for interiors, exteriors, floors, walls, pools, hospitality spaces, commercial environments, and large-scale architectural installations.

From a single statement piece to a multi-surface project, we collaborate with architects, interior designers, developers, contractors, and hospitality professionals from concept and rendering through production and delivery.

Ancient technique. Contemporary design. Made entirely for your project.

Bathrooms & wet rooms  —  installed

Mosaic belongs in wet areas.

Stone and glass were the original wet-area surface, and they still are. Set on a tanked substrate in epoxy, a mosaic goes anywhere water goes — shower walls and ceilings, steam rooms, wet-room floors, bath surrounds, hammams and spas — and stays there.

  • Water absorption ≤ 0.5% to ASTM C373
  • Epoxy adhesive and grout throughout
  • Wet-area slip resistance DCOF ≥ 0.42
  • Cut around niches, benches, drains and glass

Technical  —  III

Installation, site & care.

Any competent tiling contractor can fix mesh-backed panels. These are the conditions the work needs.

ItemRequirement
SubstrateFlat, sound, dry and free of contaminants before any mosaic is fixed.
WaterproofingRequired in showers, bathrooms, fountains and pools — a compliant tanking system applied and tested first.
AdhesiveEpoxy required for wet, outdoor and submerged work. White or light adhesive under translucent glass and pale stone.
GroutingUnsanded epoxy on glass, mandatory in wet areas and pools. Grout colour changes how the whole piece reads — sampled first.
Movement jointsMandatory at perimeters, internal corners and every change of plane; silicone, never rigid grout.
ItemRequirement
Slip resistanceDCOF ≥ 0.42 interior wet, ≥ 0.60 exterior. Honed and tumbled finishes hold more grip than polished.
SealingRequired on marble. Penetrating sealer before grouting, and on the cycle the site specifies.
CleaningpH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth. Never acidic, abrasive or bleach-based on natural stone.
Pool commissioningCure the adhesive and grout fully before filling; then balance the water before the surface is used.
DeliveryCrated by panel sequence with the setting-out key, material schedule and spare tesserae from the same batch.

From idea to installation

Seven stages, one team, no hand-offs.

01

Brief

Drawings, dimensions and references — with a site visit or a video walk-through where possible.

02

Design, sketch & rendering

The composition drawn as a master sketch, then returned as a rendering in your own space — both at no cost.

03

Material lay-up

The actual stones and glass cut and laid up physically, photographed for sign-off.

04

Cutting drawing

Setting-out lines, panel divisions and a numbered key, coordinated with the site programme.

05

Atelier production

Cut and set by hand at the bench, photographed at agreed stages so you watch it grow.

06

Delivery

Crated by panel sequence with the key, schedule and spare tesserae from the same batch.

07

Installation

Fitted by our team, or by your contractor with our drawings and remote supervision.
