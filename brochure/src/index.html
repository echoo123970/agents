The atelier

Where Roman Craft Meets
Modern Design

For over two thousand years, mosaic has transformed architecture through the meticulous placement of individual pieces of stone and glass. We preserve that traditional craftsmanship while adapting it to the scale, precision, and creative requirements of contemporary spaces.

Every project is made to order. Starting from a concept, artwork, logo, pattern, or architectural drawing, our designers develop a custom mosaic tailored to the project's exact dimensions, shape, colors, materials, and application.

Our artisans then bring the design to life one tessera at a time, using natural marble and glass to create pieces for interiors, exteriors, floors, walls, pools, hospitality spaces, commercial environments, and large-scale architectural installations.

From a single statement piece to a multi-surface project, we collaborate with architects, interior designers, developers, contractors, and hospitality professionals from concept and rendering through production and delivery.

Ancient technique. Contemporary design. Made entirely for your project.

Technical

Everything a specifier needs, on one sheet.

Figures rounded to the nearest whole unit · tested against recognised standards, with batch certificates issued on the order · every mosaic weatherproof, indoors and out

Material
Natural stone
Marble, onyx, travertine and granite, selected for vein before cutting. Thickness 5 mm.
Hand-poured glass
Full-body colour, no cut edges to oxidise. Thickness 4 mm. Metal-leaf glass with 24k gold, white gold or copper sealed between layers.
Engineered quartz
Natural quartz aggregate bound with resin. Mohs 7, water absorption approximately 0.02%. Thickness 8 mm.
Backing
Fibreglass netted mesh on every sheet and panel, so the trade fixes the tile and not the tessera.
Format
Sheet size
300 × 300 mm nominal, joint ~1 mm. Panels numbered to a setting-out key.
Weight
Glass ≈ 10 kg/m². Stone 13–15 kg/m², by thickness.
Max single panel
to confirm — larger artworks split on the drawing and numbered.
Tolerance
Sheet within 1 mm; outline within to confirm.
Applications
Walls and floors, interior and exterior, wet areas and fully submerged.
Installation
Substrate
Flat, sound, dry and free of contaminants before any mosaic is fixed.
Waterproofing
Required in showers, bathrooms, fountains and pools — a compliant tanking system applied and tested first.
Adhesive
Epoxy required for wet, outdoor and submerged work. White or light adhesive under translucent glass and pale stone.
Grouting
Unsanded epoxy on glass, mandatory in wet areas and pools. Grout colour changes how the whole piece reads — sampled first.
Movement joints
Mandatory at perimeters, internal corners and every change of plane; silicone, never rigid grout.
Site & care
Slip resistance
DCOF ≥ 0.42 interior wet, ≥ 0.60 exterior. Honed finishes hold more grip than polished.
Sealing
Required on marble. Penetrating sealer before grouting, and on the cycle the site specifies.
Cleaning
pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth. Never acidic, abrasive or bleach-based on natural stone.
Pool commissioning
Cure the adhesive and grout fully before filling; then balance the water before the surface is used.
Delivery
Crated by panel sequence with the setting-out key, material schedule and spare tesserae from the same batch.

From idea to installation

Seven stages, one team, no hand-offs.

01

Brief

Drawings, dimensions and references — with a site visit or a video walk-through where possible.

02

Design, sketch & rendering

The composition drawn as a master sketch, then returned as a rendering in your own space — both at no cost.

03

Material lay-up

The actual stones and glass cut and laid up physically, photographed for sign-off.

04

Cutting drawing

Setting-out lines, panel divisions and a numbered key, coordinated with the site programme.

05

Atelier production

Cut and set by hand at the bench, photographed at agreed stages so you watch it grow.

06

Delivery

Crated by panel sequence with the key, schedule and spare tesserae from the same batch.

07

Installation

Fitted by our team, or by your contractor with our drawings and remote supervision.
