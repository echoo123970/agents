The announcement

Our atelier has expanded — and with it, what we can make for you.

Three capabilities are now open to trade partners, and every one of them exists for a single reason: to remove the limits on what a client can ask for.

For years the constraint in mosaic work has not been skill — it has been scale, palette and time. A design had to be simplified to fit the stock, the panel sizes had to suit the workshop rather than the wall, and a bespoke colour meant a season of waiting.

We have rebuilt the studio around the opposite premise. The design desk, the stone saws, the glass and the fitting team now sit inside one process, so a commission moves from reference image to installed surface without ever being handed to a third party — and without being edited down to what was convenient to produce.

The question is no longer what we can supply.
It is what you would like made.

I
Marble and glass, fused in one composition

Hand-cut stone and hand-poured glass, including 24k gold-leaf tesserae, set in the same artwork — stone for depth and vein, glass for light and saturation.

II
Customisation without a catalogue

No fixed range to choose from. You supply artwork, a mood, a material sample or a photograph; we return a cutting drawing and a physical colour lay-up for approval.

III
Made to the millimetre of your drawing

Panels are set out to your site dimensions, numbered, and delivered with an installation key — so what leaves the atelier fits the wall it was measured for.

The detail

Thousands of tesserae, placed by hand.

Stone

Blocks are selected for vein direction before a single cut is made, so a figure runs through the finished composition rather than across it. Tesserae are split and dressed by hand — the edge you see is a cut edge, not a moulded one.

Glass

Hand-poured glass carries colour through its full body, so a chipped or cut face reveals the same tone. Gold and white-gold leaf are laid between glass layers, which is why the metal keeps its lustre in wet areas.

Setting

The flow of the setting lines — the andamento — is drawn before assembly. It is the difference between a picture made of tiles and a mosaic that moves with the light in the room.

Technical  ·  V

Care & maintenance.

One page to hand to the client or the facilities team. Followed, it keeps a mosaic looking the way it did on the day it was signed off.

Recommended

Regular cleaning

pH-neutral stone or glass cleaners, and nothing stronger for routine work.

Annual protection

A two-in-one colour enhancer and sealer applied once a year on natural stone.

Immediate spill clean-up

Wipe spills promptly — most staining on stone is a spill that was left.

Avoid

Acidic cleaners

They etch and damage natural stone surfaces, and the damage is permanent.

Abrasive products

Pads and powders scratch polished finishes and dull the shine, and they will mark metal leaf.

Harsh chemicals

Bleach and ammonia discolour grout and surfaces alike.

On hand-over

Final clean and the first seal are carried out by the installing team, and the maintenance sheet is handed over with the panel key.

Once a year

Re-apply the two-in-one colour enhancer and sealer on natural stone — more often in a shower, a pool surround or an exterior courtyard.

Before any repair

Quote the commission reference. The material record tells us which stones and which pour batch to match, decades after delivery.

Asked by specifiers

The eight questions that come up first.

Can you match a stone already on site?

Send a sample or a photograph with a colour reference. We cut a comparison lay-up and you approve the match before anything is committed.

Can you work from my client's photograph?

Yes — a photograph, a painting, a fabric, a wallpaper, a logo or a sketch on paper. The studio redraws it as a cutting design, at the scale the wall needs.

Will it arrive as loose tiles?

No. Everything is mesh-backed and numbered, so your contractor fixes panels in sequence rather than assembling an artwork on site.

Can it go in a shower, hammam or pool?

Yes, with epoxy bedding and grout over a compliant waterproofing system. Gold and white-gold leaf sit between glass layers, so submersion does not dull them.

How long does a commission take?

Design and approval 2–3 weeks, production from 4 weeks, with the programme fixed against your site dates before production starts.

Do you install, and do you ship to us?

Either — our team fits the work, or your contractor does it to our drawings with supervision from the studio. Shipping is free worldwide, crated and documented by the atelier.

Can the client see it before it is made?

Always, and the rendering is free. You see the mosaic in the room first, then the actual stone and glass laid up physically. Nothing is cut before both are signed off.

What if a piece is damaged years later?

Every commission carries a lifetime warranty. Spare tesserae from the same batch ship with the order and the material record is kept, so a repair or an extension matches the original.
