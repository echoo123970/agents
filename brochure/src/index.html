The detail

Thousands of tesserae, placed by hand.

Stone

Blocks are selected for vein direction before a single cut is made, so a figure runs through the finished composition rather than across it. Tesserae are split and dressed by hand — the edge you see is a cut edge, not a moulded one.

Glass

Hand-poured glass carries colour through its full body, so a chipped or cut face reveals the same tone. Gold and white-gold leaf are laid between glass layers, which is why the metal keeps its lustre in wet areas.

Setting

The flow of the setting lines — the andamento — is drawn before assembly. It is the difference between a picture made of tiles and a mosaic that moves with the light in the room.

The materials  ·  II

Engineered stone & hand-poured glass.

Engineered stone brings saturated, repeatable colour that natural stone cannot hold. Glass brings light: colour runs through the full body, so cut edges keep their tone, and gold, white-gold and copper leaf are sealed between layers for wet and submerged work.

Technical  ·  III

Installation, site & care.

Mesh-backed panels are fixed by any competent tiling contractor. These are the conditions the work needs from the site, and the regime that keeps it looking the way it did on hand-over.

ItemRequirement
SubstrateRequired. Clean, flat and structurally sound; dry and free of contaminants before any mosaic is fixed
WaterproofingRequired in showers, hammams, fountains and pools: a compliant tanking system, applied and tested first
AdhesiveEpoxy adhesive recommended, and required for wet, outdoor and submerged areas. White adhesive is essential beneath translucent glass and pale stone
FixingFull bed, back-buttered on larger panels, mesh pressed fully into the adhesive. Floating method where a design mixes materials of different thickness
GroutMandatory in wet and outdoor areas. Epoxy grout recommended throughout — non-porous and chemical-resistant
Movement jointsMandatory. Carried through the mosaic to the project's own layout, with silicone at every change of plane
Slip resistanceDCOF ≥ 0.42 interior wet and ≥ 0.60 exterior, to local code. Honed (R11) rather than polished (R10) where a floor gets wet
SealingRequired on marble, particularly in wet and exterior areas, after grouting
CuringFull adhesive cure before grouting, and before filling pools or fountains — allow 14–28 days depending on system and conditions
Site conditionsA controlled environment: avoid extreme temperatures during fixing and curing, and protect from direct sun and draught
ProtectionCovered and protected from following trades until hand-over; final clean by the installing team
DocumentationCutting drawing, numbered installation key, material schedule and maintenance sheet issued with every delivery
Recommended

pH-neutral stone or glass cleaners for routine work; a two-in-one colour enhancer and sealer once a year on natural stone; spills wiped promptly — most staining is a spill that was left.

Avoid

Acidic cleaners, which etch natural stone permanently; abrasive pads and powders, which dull polished finishes and mark metal leaf; bleach and ammonia, which discolour grout and surfaces alike.

Asked by specifiers

The eight questions that come up first.

Can you match a stone already on site?

Send a sample or a photograph with a colour reference. We cut a comparison lay-up and you approve the match before anything is committed.

Can you work from my client's photograph?

Yes — a photograph, a painting, a fabric, a wallpaper, a logo or a sketch on paper. The studio redraws it as a cutting design, at the scale the wall needs.

Will it arrive as loose tiles?

No. Everything is mesh-backed and numbered, so your contractor fixes panels in sequence rather than assembling an artwork on site.

Can it go in a shower, hammam or pool?

Yes, with epoxy bedding and grout over a compliant waterproofing system. Gold and white-gold leaf sit between glass layers, so submersion does not dull them.

How long does a commission take?

Design and approval 2–3 weeks, production from 4 weeks, with the programme fixed against your site dates before production starts.

Do you install, and do you ship to us?

Either — our team fits the work, or your contractor does it to our drawings with supervision from the studio. Shipping is free worldwide, crated and documented by the atelier.

Can the client see it before it is made?

Always, and the rendering is free. You see the mosaic in the room first, then the actual stone and glass laid up physically. Nothing is cut before both are signed off.

What if a piece is damaged years later?

Every commission carries a lifetime warranty. Spare tesserae from the same batch ship with the order and the material record is kept, so a repair or an extension matches the original.
